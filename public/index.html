How to play

  

    
Five political ideas a day. Place each one where you think it sits on the grid, then lock it in.

    
    
      
  • Left to right is economics. Sharing wealth and public ownership sit on the left. Free markets and low taxes sit on the right.
    • 
      
  • Top to bottom is personal freedom. Strong state control sits at the top. Individual liberty sits at the bottom.
    • 
      
  • Closer scores higher. Up to 100 points per idea, 500 for a perfect day.
    •
